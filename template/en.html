        How each deal was checked

        Every product is checked against an independent public price record, or against the price of the same model at other stores. The real discount is measured from the price the item actually sold at before the sale, not from the “before” price the seller prints.

        The headline
        1. Price record: for stores a public price record covers, the real “before” is the last price recorded for that same product before the sale. If it has been cheaper in the past, we say so.
        2. Same model elsewhere: where no record exists, the reference is the price of the same model at another Saudi store today. Such a deal is described as cheaper than another store, not as a discount, because the store did not necessarily cut its own price.
        3. No reference: when neither exists the deal is shown as “no price record” and claims nothing.
        4. Stock check, every deal, every refresh: we open the product page and read what a buyer sees. In stock = the add-to-cart button is active at the price shown here · Low stock = the store shows 3 or fewer left · Out of stock · Offer ended = the price went back up · Link broken. Stock can differ by city (checked for Riyadh).
        5. The verdict

        Limits: a price record samples prices periodically and can miss a short offer. Product names are each store’s own English names.
